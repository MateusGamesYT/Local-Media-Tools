Face recognition models from InsightFace (https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface, release v0.7),
trained on WebFace600K:
- ResNet-50 "w600k_r50" from the buffalo_l package (the Gallery's people), converted to TensorFlow
  Lite with 8-bit weights;
- MobileFaceNet "w600k_mbf" from the buffalo_s package (face blur), its weights stored as 16-bit floats.
Nothing else is changed.
