Open Images Dataset V7 — annotations
https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/index.html

The gallery's category thresholds were calibrated, and its small scene recognisers (linear
probes) were trained, using human-verified image-level labels from Open Images. The annotations
are licensed by Google LLC under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
No images from the dataset are included in this app.
