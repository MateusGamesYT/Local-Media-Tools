Lists of common first names and surnames (assets/privacy/first_names.txt, surnames.txt), used only to
recognise names in text the app reads, so they can be blurred. Made by buildtools/privacy/make_names.py
from the most common names of three sources, written in lower case without accents:

- United States: baby names recorded by the US Social Security Administration (public data),
  compiled as "US SSA Baby Names (National)" by nkrishnaswami on Hugging Face, CC0 1.0.

- Brazil: "IBGE Nomes no Brasil 2022", data of the Instituto Brasileiro de Geografia e Estatística
  (IBGE, Censo Demográfico 2022), compiled and structured by Gabriel Lobo (@olob0) on Hugging Face,
  licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changes: only the most common given names and surnames, lower case, without accents.

- France: "French First Names from Death Records (1970-2024)", data of INSEE, compiled by Ronan Le
  Meillat on Hugging Face (eltorio/french_first_names_insee_2024), MIT License:
